# Photo guide for the sorghum type test

This page is for the team taking the photos. The rules come from the frozen protocol
([type-evaluation-protocol.md](architecture/type-evaluation-protocol.md), section 7). If this
page and the protocol ever disagree, the protocol is right.

## What you need

- A phone with the sorghum app installed (the build named in the session plan).
- A **matte grey board**, about A3 size. It must not be shiny, white or patterned.
- The **ColorChecker Classic Mini** colour card. Keep it clean, dry and out of the sun.
- A **ruler marked in mm**, at least 15 cm long.
- The seed packet or plant tag with the **accession number**, for example `PI 562744`.
- Daylight in the shade, or an overcast sky. Do not use flash or direct sun.

## Every photo, every time

1. Put the board flat and the sample in the middle.
2. Put the colour card and the ruler on the board, next to the sample, without touching it.
   Both must be fully in the picture.
3. Check that no shadow falls across the sample or the card, including your own.
4. Hold the phone upright (portrait) over the board. Follow the app until it says the phone is
   level, then hold still until it takes the photo.
5. If the app says the photo is blurred, too dark or has no card, take it again. Never keep a
   photo the app rejected.
6. Before you move to the next accession, check that the accession number in the app is right.

## The views for each accession

| View | How many | What to put on the board |
|---|---|---|
| **Panicle** | 1 per plant, from **2 different plants** | The whole dry head, laid flat. Turn it so its widest side faces the camera |
| **Grain** | 1 | About 50 threshed grains in one layer, not touching each other |
| **Glume** | 1 | 10 to 20 spikelets taken from the middle of the head, with the grain still inside the glumes. Get close: the glumes should fill most of the picture, with the card and ruler at the edge |
| **Leaf** (optional) | 1 per plant | The middle part of the top leaf (flag leaf), upper side up, with the midrib along the board |

The leaf view is optional because a leaf does not show the race. Take it when you have time,
because it helps the app learn leaf colour traits and recognise sorghum.

## Test accessions

Some accessions are marked **test** on your list. Photograph them exactly like the others, in the
same sessions, and mixed in with them. The only difference is where the photos go: send test photos
**only to the custodian** named on the list, and delete them from the phone after the custodian
confirms they arrived. Do not share them, look at them in the model tools, or put them on the
shared drive. One leaked test photo can make the whole test count for nothing.

## Do not

- Mix two accessions on one board, or move the label between plants.
- Edit, crop or filter a photo, or send it through a chat app that compresses it. Copy the
  original files.
- Photograph a plant whose head is still green or wet. Wait until it is dry.
- Guess an accession number. If a tag is lost, set the plant aside and tell the custodian.
